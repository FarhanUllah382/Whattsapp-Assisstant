🛡️ Database Security Architecture & Controls

1. Anti-IDOR (Insecure Direct Object Reference) Protection

Internal sequential primary keys (orders.id) are never accepted from or shown to customers. Orders are referenced externally solely by public_ref — an 80-bit cryptographically random token ('ORD-' || upper(hex(randomblob(10)))) validated by database check constraints and triggers.

2. Customer Identity Scoping & Anti-Brute-Force Rate Limiting

Order lookups require both the public token and matching authenticated customer ID (where public_ref = ? and customer_id = ?). All attempts are recorded in order_lookup_attempts with SHA-256 hashed queries; 10 failed queries within a 15-minute sliding window triggers an automatic lockout.

3. Double-Entry Accounting & Exact-Money Protection

Floats are eliminated. Monetary fields use non-negative integers in Pakistani paisas. The mutable balance_owed column was dropped — customer balances are derived dynamically from the immutable ledger. Stock cannot go negative due to database-level abort triggers.

4. PII Minimization & Segregation (Correction #5)

Delivery information (phone numbers, full addresses) is strictly confined to authoritative fulfillment tables (orders, order_drafts). Derived LLM memories (checkpoints, customer_notes) are actively sanitized via sanitizeMemoryText, tracked idempotently via privacy_migrations.

5. Replay Defense & Distributed Idempotency

inbound_guard_state and inbound_events drop duplicate webhooks and isolate offline backlogs on restart. send_ledger prevents duplicate WhatsApp sends if the Node process restarts midway through a turn.
