## Delivery

We deliver all over Pakistan.

Orders are normally processed Monday to Friday during business hours.
Delivery usually takes 3-5 business days after order confirmation, though
this can vary depending on the customer's location.

Delivery is free all over Pakistan.

If a specific delivery situation isn't covered here, confirm with Ahmed
before promising a delivery date or charge.

## Return Policy

Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery, if the customer contacts
us within that window.

To be eligible for a return, the product must be unused and unworn, in
its original condition, with original tags and packaging still attached.
Items that have been worn, washed, damaged, or altered aren't eligible.

If a return situation isn't clearly covered by this policy, don't invent
an answer — confirm with Ahmed.

## Exchange Policy

We allow exchanges for eligible products, including size exchanges,
within 7 days of delivery — same conditions as returns: unused, unworn,
original condition, with tags and packaging still attached. The
replacement size or product must be confirmed as available via live
stock before promising it.

A customer may request an exchange for a different product if it meets
the same conditions and Ahmed approves it. If the requested replacement
isn't available, don't promise it — confirm with Ahmed.
